**Status meanings:** **Gap replayed** means the originally missing application interaction was observed within the method and boundary stated in that row; it does not turn a fake provider or transport into a real one. **Partial** identifies a remaining original scope or planned subcase. **Blocked** requires unavailable infrastructure.
